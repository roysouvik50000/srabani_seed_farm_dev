Our Foundation

Rooted in Sabang, Dedicated to Farmer Prosperity

Agriculture is the backbone of rural livelihood in West Bengal. At SRABANI SEED FARM, we believe every good harvest begins beneath the soil with the integrity of the seed.

Located along the Sabang - Moharh Road in Sindurmuri, Sabang (Paschim Medinipur, West Bengal 721144), SRABANI SEED FARM has established itself as a reputable agricultural seed supplier. We specialize in producing, processing, and distributing tested paddy seeds tailored to regional agricultural needs.

By combining practical farming insight with systematic cleaning and sorting methods, we provide growers with seed varieties that exhibit uniform germination, dependable seedling vigour, and regional resilience.

Our Guiding Principles

Mission & Vision

The core standards that shape every seed lot we prepare and distribute.

Our Mission

To provide farmers in Sabang and across West Bengal with superior, physically pure, and high-vitality paddy seeds. We aim to protect farmer investment by ensuring that every bag leaving our facility adheres to strict grading, proper moisture retention, and dependable germination standards.

Our Vision

To build a resilient and prosperous agricultural community in rural Bengal by serving as the region's most trusted partner in paddy seed supply — championing both high-demand commercial varieties like Pousami Gold and celebrated aromatic heritage varieties like Gobinda Bhog and Badsha Bhog.

Pledge to the Land

Our Commitment to Farmers

We know the labor, expense, and anticipation that go into every sowing season. That is why our commitments are clear and non-negotiable.

Varietal Integrity

We maintain true-to-type seed stocks. Our heritage aromatic varieties like Gobinda Bhog and Badsha Bhog are safeguarded against accidental cross-contamination so their aroma and cooking traits remain authentic.

Honest Communication

We never fabricate laboratory yield claims or exaggerated statistics. We communicate factual seed characteristics, field performance observations, and clear handling recommendations.

Direct Grower Access

Farmers and local agricultural retailers can visit our Sindurmuri farm directly in Sabang or speak with us by phone at any time for sowing guidance and seasonal lot availability.

Quality Workflow

How We Prepare Our Seeds

Careful processing steps designed to maximize field germination and purity.

1

Source Selection

Identifying healthy standing parent crops with robust tillers and no weed contamination.

2

Thorough Cleaning

Mechanical and manual screening to remove lightweight chaff, dust, and inert materials.

3

Grading & Sizing

Classifying grains by uniform density and dimensions for steady seed nursery emergence.

4

Sealed Packaging

Packed in secure farm bags to preserve embryo viability and prevent moisture damage.
