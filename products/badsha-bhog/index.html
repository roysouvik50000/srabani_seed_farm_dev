Badsha Bhog Paddy Seed

Badsha Bhog Paddy Seed from SRABANI SEED FARM.

Badsha Bhog is a celebrated traditional scented paddy variety cultivated across Bengal. Recognized for its sweet floral scent and slender-medium grain texture, SRABANI SEED FARM provides genuine Badsha Bhog seeds that undergo systematic grading and seed lot monitoring to support farmers cultivating premium fragrant rice.

Key Variety Features

  • Prized Bengal heritage scented rice variety
  • Distinct sweet fragrance and slender grain structure
  • Strict grading ensures uniform seed density and size
  • Direct from Sabang, Paschim Medinipur distribution
