About Us

Our journey in sustainable agriculture

Founded in 2005, GreenHarvest Seeds has been at the forefront of agricultural innovation for over 20 years. We specialize in developing high-quality paddy seeds that help farmers maximize their yields while practicing sustainable agriculture.

Our team of agricultural scientists and researchers work tirelessly to develop seed varieties that are not only high-yielding but also resistant to pests, diseases, and adverse weather conditions.
